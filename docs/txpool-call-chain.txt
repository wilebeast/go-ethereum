Expanded Notes
==============

1. TxPool.Add
   - Entry point for the unified txpool.
   - Splits incoming transactions by subpool type.

2. LegacyPool.Add
   - Filters known and basically invalid transactions.
   - Calls addTxsLocked, which eventually calls add.

3. add
   - Performs stateful validation and replacement / overflow logic.
   - Decides whether the tx replaces a pending tx or should enter the future queue.

4. enqueueTx
   - Inserts the transaction into the queue structure.
   - Updates all/priced indexes.

5. requestPromoteExecutables
   - Schedules async promotion work through the reorg loop.

6. runReorg
   - Central async maintenance stage.
   - Handles reset, promotion, demotion, truncation, and tx event emission.

7. promoteExecutables
   - Pulls contiguous executable txs out of the future queue.
   - Moves them into pending.

8. Pending
   - Exposes executable txs as account-grouped, nonce-ordered LazyTransactions.

9. miner
   - Reads Pending(filter).
   - Builds a per-account head heap ordered by effective tip / time.
   - Commits transactions into the block candidate.
